\paragraph{Deep-baseline record.} The archived scoring chain is \texttt{run\_modern.py} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{bmlib.anomalies} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{cc\_lib.anomaly}. Only TRAIN rows are phase-centred by the called implementation, leaving VAL/TEST raw. Deduplicating Porto GWNET fold 0 by retaining the final authoritative per-date record gives an archived single draw of 0.4970176832 in Porto; pooling the archived San Francisco per-date numerators and denominators gives 0.2540477278. These are the values used in main Table~1, not the sealed seed means. Graphormer's 60-epoch budget and incomplete fold-level prediction-file export remain disclosed. Per-date error records exist for all scored archived folds, but zero-divergence residuals follow from projection and are not learned conservation.

\paragraph{Release scope and unresolved checks.} The public export separates figure rebuilding from frozen products, core-score recomputation and end-to-end raw-trajectory reconstruction. It contains no individual-level trajectory records. Large prediction files, unavailable original training artifacts and incomplete sealed per-run outputs are stated rather than represented as included. The matched-capacity construction modules are included, but the sweep remains dependent on external aligned fields, date-freeze files and GIS rasters listed in the data manifest. Raw-data redistribution requirements, the OSM snapshot/version and per-artifact licence notices remain release checks (Note~\ref{note:data}). Public repository location: \repositorylocation.
